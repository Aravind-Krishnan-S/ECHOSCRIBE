# Role-play recording protocol

1. **Consent.** Both role-players sign a short consent form: the recording is used to evaluate
   EchoScribe, may be processed by the cloud model providers configured for the study (name them),
   and contains no real personal information.
2. **Scenarios.** Use invented personas only. Therapy (12): sleep and work stress, grief, social
   anxiety, relationship conflict, panic, low mood (include 3 where the client mentions passive
   death wishes, 1 with resolved past ideation, so the risk screen sees realistic cases). Mentoring
   (8): exam stress, thesis delays, career choice, time management, burnout (2).
3. **Setting.** A quiet room for 10 sessions; a room with background noise (fan, cafe audio at low
   volume) for 10. One laptop microphone, 1–1.5 m from both speakers.
4. **Length.** 5–10 minutes. The client should speak more than the professional in at least 6
   sessions (tests role resolution when talk time does not identify the professional).
5. **Files.** `S01_therapy_quiet.wav`, … 16 kHz or higher. Keep a one-line scenario summary per file.
6. **Processing.** Upload each file through EchoScribe once with the default settings and export
   the JSON (summary page → Export record). Keep the provider names that served each stage.
