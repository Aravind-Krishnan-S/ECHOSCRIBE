\textbf{What the results support.} Citation-constrained writing kept almost every sentence attached to evidence, and an independent vendor caught most planted factual errors, so sentence-level flags give a reviewer a concrete place to look instead of an unexplained confidence score~\cite{guo2017calibration}. The router results show that resilience comes from remembering failures, and that the choice of strategy is a trade-off between staying on the preferred model and minimising latency, not a single best setting. Role identification without enrolment was accurate on two-party clinical dialogue, and the combination of statistics and model was more accurate than either alone.

\textbf{Deployment.} EchoScribe runs as a local application: the server, the speech service and the database client run on the clinician's machine, and only the configured model vendors receive audio or text. Keys stay in the browser unless the user chooses to sync one. Sessions are not limited in length; long recordings are compressed to mono Opus before they reach a cloud model, speaker separation runs locally (on a GPU when present), and timeouts scale with duration. Because model access is per role and per vendor, an institution can restrict processing to vendors its data-governance policy approves, and speaker separation never leaves the machine unless the local service is unavailable.

\textbf{Limitations.} (i) ACI-Bench contains medical visits, not therapy or mentoring sessions; no public corpus of counselling sessions with reference notes exists, and GROW notes were not evaluated quantitatively. (ii) Support labels come from a model; the planted-error study bounds how often it misses errors of three kinds, but subtler omissions and misattributions need human review~\cite{asgari2025}. (iii) Speech results come from simulated conversations of read speech without overlap; spontaneous, overlapping clinical speech will be harder. (iv) Results depend on specific model versions that vendors retire; we log the serving model of every request. (v) The risk screen has not been validated against clinician labels. The protocol released with the code covers these gaps: blinded ratings of evidence-linked against single-pass notes by clinicians and mentors, annotated role-played sessions for real-speech WER, DER and role accuracy, and a clinician-labelled vignette probe of the risk screen.
