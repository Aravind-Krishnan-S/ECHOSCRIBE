\textbf{Limitations.} (i) ACI-Bench and PriMock57 are medical, and AnnoMI has no reference notes; no public corpus of counselling or mentoring sessions with reference notes exists\ifmentoring, and the mentoring sessions we ran are acted and share personas\else, so GROW notes were not evaluated\fi. (ii) Support labels come from a model; the planted-error test bounds how often it misses errors of three kinds, but subtler omissions and misattributions need human review~\cite{asgari2025}. (iii) Simulated speech is read without overlap, and PriMock57 is acted speech; overlapping, emotional clinical speech will be harder, and at 0~dB our attribution is not better than cloud diarization. (iv) Results depend on specific model versions that vendors retire; we log the serving model of every request. (v) The risk screen has not been validated against clinician labels. Blinded rating kits for notes and verifier agreement are released with the code. \textbf{Ethics:} no patient or clinical data were used; experiments use public datasets, simulated speech and scripted role-plays recorded by volunteers who knew the audio would be processed by the system, and free-tier model vendors may use submitted text for training, so deployments with real sessions should use institutionally approved vendors or local models.
